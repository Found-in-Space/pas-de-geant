Earth landscapes

Visit mountains and deserts, then watch the boundary between day and night move. Select a chapter to travel. Explore freely stops the guided motion at the current view; choose another chapter to rejoin.

Starting case…

In VR, selecting a chapter starts continuous movement of the world around you. Head tracking remains free. This is a development example awaiting physical-headset comfort review.

In VR, use the panel on your left controller with the right-hand pointer. Explore freely stops the journey at your current view. Chapter arrival holds a stationary view; continuous travel begins only when you select a chapter.

Default imagery: NASA Blue Marble Next Generation (July 2004) and Black Marble (2016). These are historical composites. The final chapter advances simulation time at 600×; it does not show live city lights or weather.

The landscape chapters choose a fixed daylight time for each region. The final chapter starts at a fixed UTC and advances the day/night boundary. These lighting choices do not change the dates of the source imagery.

Preparing surface layers…

Each chapter waits for its requested detail after the camera arrives. If source data is unavailable, this example continues with an explicit degraded readout. Readiness covers the current view and the source's available resolution; it does not guarantee coverage along the whole route.
