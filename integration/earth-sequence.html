The orange marker and its path are authored illustrations, not a satellite prediction. Its displayed radius is about 76 km. The fixed world epoch is 30 October 2026 at 12:00 UTC; presentation seconds do not advance that clock.

NASA Blue Marble (July 2004), Black Marble (2016), and measured Mapterhorn elevation provide the same Earth defaults as the chapter example. Camera offsets do not modify those data.

In VR, use the panel on your left controller with the right-hand pointer. Rejoin moves into the shot; Play then starts continuous tracking. Pause holds the current sample, and Explore freely releases it. Head movement remains free. Physical Quest comfort and panel usability review remain pending.
