XR movement models

Open on a headset through localhost or HTTPS. Select a model, then select Enter VR. In surface mode, use the left stick to travel. Use the vertical right stick to change physical altitude. In observation mode, the same inputs move the surface location and change the map height. The map must stay fixed when you move your head.

In ship mode, use the right stick to travel. Use the left stick for pitch and yaw. Hold the left grip for roll. Hold the right grip for faster travel. Looking around must not steer the ship. Release the inputs after each model or session change.
