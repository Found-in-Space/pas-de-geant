Earth day and night

Orbit Earth to explore the terminator and city lights. Drag to orbit and use the wheel to approach. In VR, the left stick travels across the globe and the vertical right stick changes altitude above the ground, bringing Earth closer or farther away. The demo starts at the current time.

Simulation UTC:

NASA Blue Marble Next Generation (July 2004) and NASA Earth Observatory / NOAA NGDC Black Marble (2016). These are static historical composites. The night image includes background geography and city lights; it is not a calibrated emission map. The Sun direction and Earth rotation follow the simulation time; the soft twilight blend is illustrative. Clouds, weather and changing light use are not simulated.

Both layers stream 256-pixel tiles from Found in Space. The bundled low-resolution Earth remains available when disconnected; unavailable night imagery stays dark.
