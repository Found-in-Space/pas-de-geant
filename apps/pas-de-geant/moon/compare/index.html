Preview method & other sources

The two layers share a geographic lunar grid on the 1,737.4 km reference sphere. Transparent areas reveal the WAC base. The shared curve applies equally to both layers; it reproduces the viewer’s linear-light brightness exponent, without 3D lighting or ACES exposure.

Use this page to shortlist imagery, then verify the chosen archived TIFF before admitting it to a texture build. The service previews are not raw measurements.

Brightness matching changes display gain in linear light using the median of corresponding terrain ratios. It preserves texture contrast; it is a visual match, not a calibrated albedo measurement. High-Sun observations usually make brightness comparisons easier.

Regional previews use the archive’s native-resolution tiled mosaics. Their footprints follow the published 20 m browse rasters’ no-data masks, so outer edges are approximate at that scale. Coverage varies by site; a square inspection area does not imply complete NAC coverage.

Chandrayaan-2 OHRC is available through ISSDC ↗. Its calibrated observations need a registered image crop before they can be compared here.
