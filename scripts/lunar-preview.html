Resolution, contrast & provenance

The preview is opaque. Clipped dark pixels display as black and clipped bright pixels as white. Blank source data and the area outside each crop also display as black; no terrain detail is invented or filled from another dataset. Downloadable GeoTIFFs retain their scientific validity masks. Grid spacing describes sampling, not guaranteed optical resolution. These selected areas are samples, not a global coverage audit.

1:1 maps one compiled image pixel to one physical display pixel. Equatorial images retain native sampling; polar images are reprojected to a common 10 m grid. Zooming farther adds no detail. Modified locally by Found in Space for this comparison.
